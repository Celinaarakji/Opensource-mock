# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a friendly place for everyone, especially people
making their first open-source contribution.

**Do:**

- Be patient and welcoming. Everyone was a beginner once.
- Give feedback about the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

**Don't:**

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately.
- Make jokes or comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Share other people's private information.

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, tell a maintainer privately.
Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block people who
don't follow this code.
